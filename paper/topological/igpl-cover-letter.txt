Re: Submission of “Topological Semantics for Scoped Computational Paths”

Dear Ms. Spurr,

On behalf of my coauthors, I am pleased to submit the manuscript “Topological
Semantics for Scoped Computational Paths” for consideration for publication in
the Logic Journal of the IGPL.

The manuscript develops a topological semantics for scoped computational-path
presentations.  Its main contributions are:

1. a coherent representative carrier equipped with both a full
   trace-sensitive topology and its observable semantic coarsening, together
   with the continuous comparison between them;
2. a scoped quotient and final-domain topological groupoid construction whose multiplication is
   continuous without an additional product-quotient assumption;
3. an exact four-way criterion comparing the final composable-pair topology
   with the ordinary pullback topology, together with useful sufficient
   conditions;
4. a universal presentation theorem, a comparison and completeness interface,
   and a normal-form certificate; and
5. applications to finite-generator circle and genuine torus presentations,
   supported by a Lean development checking the principal scoped-rewrite,
   topology-comparison, and finite-generator interfaces.

Relative to our earlier work on the computational-path calculus, higher
coherence, and individual fundamental-group calculations, this manuscript
supplies the general continuous quotient semantics, the exact comparison
between final and ordinary composable-pair topologies, and a reusable
soundness-and-completeness interface.

We believe the paper is well suited to the journal’s interests in logic,
rewriting, type theory, logic and computation, and formalized mathematics.  The
paper is 30 pages in the submitted PDF.  The accompanying Lean 4.24.0 artifact,
version 0.6.1+lean-only-v3, is available through the version-specific Zenodo
record https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21938980 and permanent concept DOI
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21817207, with the corresponding source in the
ComputationalPathsLean repository:
https://github.com/Arthur742Ramos/ComputationalPathsLean.

The manuscript is original, has not been published previously, and is not
under consideration by another journal.  All authors have read and approved
the submitted version.
