Re: Topological Semantics for Scoped Computational Paths

Dear Ms. Spurr,

Please consider the enclosed manuscript, “Topological Semantics for Scoped
Computational Paths,” for publication in the Logic Journal of the IGPL. It
studies continuous geometric realizations of finite computational paths while
retaining the rewrite rules declared by each presentation.

The paper constructs a quotient groupoid whose multiplication is continuous
on the quotient of composable representatives. It then gives an exact
criterion for that topology to agree with the ordinary pullback topology on
composable quotient arrows. A separate completeness criterion identifies
when the scoped rewrite classes capture all represented homotopy classes.
The paper applies these results to the universal presentation, to based
circle and torus presentations, and to products. A complete presentation
over the harmonic archipelago shows that completeness alone does not make
the trace-sensitive and observable quotient topologies agree.

The topological inputs and winding calculations are classical. The paper's
contribution is the scoped presentation framework, its quotient-topology
comparison, and the completeness and trace-section criteria. The manuscript
distinguishes the mathematical arguments from the Lean checks and from the
earlier, narrower result registered in Palomar. The current Lean source is
available at
https://github.com/Arthur742Ramos/TopologicalComputationalPaths/tree/09b20abe0a4f69b361846c2466bff8528044fa93
and builds with Lean 4.32.0 using the repository's checked-in dependency
manifest. An earlier version of the paper is available as the preprint
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04228.

AI-assisted tools were used for editorial support and assistance with
software, documentation, and figure preparation, as disclosed in the
manuscript's Acknowledgements. The authors remain responsible for the
mathematical claims and final text.
