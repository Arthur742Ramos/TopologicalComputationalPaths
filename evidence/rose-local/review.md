# Independent review of the geometric rose milestone

The actual geometric carrier, Cayley-edge covering, lifted-word completeness,
and explicit based quotient topologies were reviewed by agents who did not
own the corresponding source files. The reduced-word prefix module was
independently reviewed by both the geometry and lifted-word authors.

The review checked the quotient topology of the interval-glued carrier,
continuous translation, separated vertex and edge neighborhoods, actual
covering fibers, explicit signed lifts, scoped normalization soundness, and
homotopy invariance of lifted endpoints. It found no circular completeness
premise or binary/flat topology identification.

Two manuscript findings were repaired: raw coherent carriers were not claimed
to be discrete (the matching flat elimination homeomorphisms give discreteness
only after scoped quotient), and the obsolete claim that the geometric rose
and covering were still future work was replaced by the precise remaining
simple-connectivity obligation.

The checked milestone concerns homotopies between represented finite traces
and the fixed-endpoint flat observable/full-word computational quotients.
It proves actual geometric ab versus ba separation. It does not classify
arbitrary rose loops, prove a global-fiber/subspace comparison, or infer the
ordinary quotient fundamental-group topology from these discrete carriers.

The next-stage CayleyFlatWalk source is uncompiled and excluded from this
publication and its replay selection.
